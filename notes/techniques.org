#+TITLE: Techniques & Patterns
# Ideas that transfer between problems. Each entry: the pattern, the cue that
# should trigger it, and the problems that taught it. This is the file worth
# turning into SRS cards (org-fc / org-drill) later, not individual problems.
# Topic deep-dives (e.g. [[file:greedy.org][greedy]]) stay in their own files.

* Complete search
** Small constraints mean try everything
- Cue :: N tiny or sum of N tiny (<= ~100, permutations of <= 8 things).
- Seen in :: [[file:../usaco/chinabull.org][Bull in a China Shop]], [[file:../ccc/twentyfour.org][Twenty Four]], [[file:../cf/threelogos.org][Three Logos]], [[file:../cf/kthprefixmex.org][Swap for k-th Prefix MEX]], [[file:../usaco/backandforth.org][Back and Forth]]
** Candidates are encoded in the input
- The only interesting cut points / rays / values are the input's own coordinates; walk them in sorted order and update incrementally instead of recounting.
- Seen in :: [[file:../usaco/balancingsilver.org][Load Balancing Silver]]

* Greedy
** Place the forced choice as late as possible
- When forced to cover a chain of dependencies, put the choice where it can still help later cells.
- Seen in :: [[file:../usaco/astral.org][Astral Superposition]]
** Serve whoever is ready first
- Seen in :: [[file:../log.org::*USACO :: Why Did the Cow Cross the Road III][Cow Cross the Road III]]

* Sorting
** Sort, then one pass with a count of what remains
- Seen in :: [[file:../log.org::*USACO :: Cow College][Cow College]]
** Compare against the sorted array
- Mismatched positions vs sorted order tell you how many moves/swaps are needed.
- Seen in :: [[file:../usaco/outofplace.org][Out of Place]]

* Observation / reduction
** Look for an invariant before simulating
- Parity, counts, or a max over pairs often collapses the whole problem.
- Seen in :: [[file:../cf/frogjumps.org][Frog Jumps]], [[file:../usaco/guessanimal.org][Cow Guessing]], [[file:../usaco/triangles.org][Triangles]]
