#+TITLE: Notes on Greedy Algorithms

** In a nutshell:
Greedy algorithms rely on making what appears to be the best choice at every step / iteration of the algorithm (a "locally optimal" choice) and then relying on this *locally optimal* choice selection manifesting as the globally optimal solution.

Greedy algorithms have some pros and cons.
Pros:
- Usually simple to implement as there is one "choice" in each step
- Usually efficient since a greedy algorithm constructs the right answer step-by-step (locally optimal -> globally optimal)

Cons:
- It is usually hard to prove the correctness of a greedy algorithm. In particular, it’s hard to translate the locally optimal choice to the globally optimal structure
