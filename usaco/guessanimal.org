# The solution in your own words, written after closing the editorial.
The most yes’ that can be said is related to the highest intersection of traits any pair of animals has. When disambiguating this pair, there will be K yes’ said where K is the number of intersecting traits, and then 1 more yes to disambiguate. So the answer is K + 1.
* Insight
The max amount of questions you can ask before reaching a one guess situation is the max amount of overlapping traits between a pair of animals
