* Insight
Stars can disappear, so gray means at least one star, not exactly one. Place stars forced by black pixels, then scan so each source comes before the cell it shifts onto; when a gray pixel is unexplained, put the star on the pixel itself (latest possible), never on the source.

* Cue

* Mistake
